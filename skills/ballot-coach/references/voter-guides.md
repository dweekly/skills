# Common voter guides

Offer the ones that fit the user's jurisdiction, and ask the research agents to find local
equivalents. Describe each one neutrally, with its known leaning, so the user can rate it with
eyes open.

## Nonpartisan or information-first (usually no recommendations)
- **Official state voter guide** (California: Secretary of State, with LAO analysis): text,
  fiscal analysis and official arguments.
- **County elections office:** impartial analyses and arguments for local measures.
- **CalMatters voter guide** (California), **Ballotpedia**, **Vote411** (LWV's site): explainers
  and candidate answers.

## Civic groups with recommendations
- **League of Women Voters** (state and local chapters): good-government and voting-access
  leaning; often takes no position on candidates.
- **SPUR** (Bay Area): urbanist, pro-housing, policy-research leaning.
- **Pete Rates the Propositions** (peterates.com, California, since 1980): one person's yes/no on every statewide proposition, with short summaries and a detailed version. Statewide propositions only, no local measures. No stated leaning; judge it by its track record against your own views.
- **GrowSF** (San Francisco): moderate, pro-housing, pro-public-safety.
- **Common Cause / ACLU:** voting-rights and civil-liberties leaning.
- **Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association / CalTax / local taxpayer associations:** generally
  oppose new taxes.
- **Chambers of commerce / CalChamber:** business leaning.
- **Labor federations / teachers' unions (e.g. CA Labor Federation, CTA):** labor leaning.
- **Environmental groups (Sierra Club, CA Environmental Voters):** environmental leaning.
- **YIMBY groups (e.g. California YIMBY):** pro-housing-supply.

## Parties
- **State and county party slates** (Democratic, Republican, Green, Libertarian and others).

## Newspaper editorial boards
- The major regional papers (California: SF Chronicle, LA Times, Mercury News, Sacramento Bee, SD
  Union-Tribune, OC Register) and local papers. Note that some have stopped endorsing.

Ask the user to add any other guide, person or newsletter they actually look to.
