# Research brief (paste into each research agent, filled in)

You are researching part of a real ballot for a voter in **{county, state}** for the
**{date}** election. Today is **{today}**. You do not know the voter's views, and you shouldn't
guess them. Steelman every side equally.

Your slice of the ballot:
{list of contests/measures exactly as printed, with choices}

Use WebSearch/WebFetch. Prefer primary sources:
- **State measures:** the official voter guide text, the legislative or fiscal analyst's analysis
  (California: LAO) and the full legal text.
- **Local measures:** the county elections office's impartial analysis and the arguments for and
  against.
- **Candidates:** their own statements, plus reputable nonpartisan guides (CalMatters,
  Ballotpedia, Vote411).
- **Context:** quality local and regional reporting.

For each **measure**, report:
- **What it does,** in plain English, compared with current law, including what the ballot label
  leaves out.
- **Fiscal analysis:** the official numbers.
- **Strongest case for and against.** Steelman both, and note who makes each case.
- **Who's behind each side:** funders with dollar amounts, endorsers and polling.
- **Interactions with other measures on this ballot:** conflicts, "whichever gets more yes votes
  wins" clauses, and measures written to defeat another.
- **Vote threshold** (majority, 55%, two-thirds).

For each **candidate contest**, report:
- **What the office actually controls,** including any recent change to its powers.
- **Each candidate:** background, top positions, record, controversies (with sources),
  endorsements, money and polling.
- **For judges:** who appointed them, any discipline, and any organized opposition.

Also collect what common **voter guides** recommend for your slice: party slates, LWV (which
often takes no position), SPUR or the local civic-research equivalent, major editorial boards,
taxpayer groups, labor federations, chambers of commerce, ACLU, GrowSF and similar groups. Record
these as data only, with links.

Rules: cite a URL for every factual claim; mark anything unverified as **unverified**; flag
conflicts between sources rather than resolving them silently; don't write files outside a
scratch directory.
Return a structured report per item.
